## Choosing a Region

- **Trading / MEV** - choose the region closest to your validator or co-located infrastructure
- **General development** - any active region works, pick the one closest to your users
- **Co-location** - we recommend Amsterdam (DigitalOcean) for VPS hosting alongside Solami infrastructure
