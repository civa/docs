ShredStream delivers raw shreds directly from validators, giving you the absolute lowest latency access to Solana data - before blocks are even confirmed.

## Features

- Direct validator shred delivery
- Sub-block latency
- Configure destination IP and port
- Multiple region support

## Configuration

Each ShredStream requires:

- **Destination IP** - your server's public IP
- **Destination Port** - UDP port to receive shreds
- **Region** - closest PoP to your infrastructure

Manage your streams in the [Dashboard](https://solami.fast/dashboard/shredstream).
