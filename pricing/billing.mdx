## Account Balance

Your Solami account has a SOL balance used to pay for subscriptions and add-ons.

### Depositing SOL

1. Go to **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Copy your unique deposit address and memo
3. Send SOL to the deposit address with the provided memo
4. Balance updates automatically once the transaction confirms

<Warning>Always include the memo when depositing. Deposits without a memo cannot be credited automatically.</Warning>

## Subscriptions

### Upgrading

Upgrade your plan from **Dashboard → Billing → Upgrade Plan**. You can pay with:

- **Account balance** - deducted instantly
- **Stripe** - redirected to Stripe checkout

### Extending

Extend your current plan before it expires from the billing page.

### Canceling

Cancel your subscription to prevent auto-renewal. Your plan remains active until the expiry date.

## Transaction History

View all transactions in the billing dashboard:

- Subscription payments
- SOL top-ups
- ShredStream purchases
- Extra gRPC connection purchases
- Dedicated server payments
- Referral withdrawals

## Stripe Portal

For Stripe-paid subscriptions, access the Stripe customer portal to manage payment methods and view invoices.
