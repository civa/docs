## Billing Cycles

Plans can be purchased with the following billing cycles:

- **Monthly**
- **Quarterly**
- **Semi-Annual**
- **Annual**

## Payment Methods

- **Account Balance** - deposit SOL to your Solami account and pay from your balance
- **Stripe** - pay with credit card via Stripe checkout
