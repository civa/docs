Add-ons are purchased independently of your plan and billed from your account balance.

## Extra gRPC Connections

Add more gRPC streaming connections beyond what your plan includes.

| Period  | Price per Connection |
|---------|---------------------|
| Daily   | $10/day             |
| Monthly | $100/month          |

Maximum 100 extra connections. Manage in [Dashboard → gRPC](https://solami.fast/dashboard/grpc).

## ShredStream

Receive raw shreds directly from validators for the lowest latency.

| Period  | Price per Stream |
|---------|-----------------|
| Daily   | $20/day         |
| Monthly | $200/month      |

Available in AMS, FRA, and NYC. Manage in [Dashboard → ShredStream](https://solami.fast/dashboard/shredstream).
